Install

Used once to sign in. It is not saved. Open this page with https:// on port 8741 (accept the certificate once). Scan the label opens the live camera on that same address, reads the QR code, and fills the password. TUA then sets the admin password to Enter@qua1!, writes the firmware and applies the config at 192.168.1.1, then treats 192.168.100.1 answering as confirmation that the config uploaded.
